en_nonwords.txt — control items for the vocabulary level test.

None of these is an English word. They were built from Latinate stems and real
English suffixes so that they have the shape of genuine low-frequency English,
and every one was checked against dwyl/english-words (words_alpha, ~370,000
entries) and rejected if it appeared there, contained a real word of five or
more letters, or ended in one. No two share a stem.

They exist because a yes/no vocabulary test taken at face value measures
confidence rather than knowledge. Saying "yes" to these is the only way to see
how much of the rest of the answers is overclaiming, and the score is corrected
by that rate.
