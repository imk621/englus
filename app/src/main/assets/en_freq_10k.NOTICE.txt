en_freq_10k.txt — top 10,000 English words by frequency, rank order.

Derived from hermitdave/FrequencyWords (2018 English list), which is built from
OpenSubtitles and distributed under the MIT License.
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords

Filtered to alphabetic tokens (internal apostrophes allowed) and de-duplicated.
Used to place a word in a frequency band, which is the standard basis for
estimating how hard a text is.
